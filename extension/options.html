Escape hatches

These are deliberately limited, because an unlimited way out is just a dismiss button:

  • Snooze — 5 minutes, once per break.
  • Emergency skip — 2 per day, reset at midnight.
  • In a meeting — holds breaks for an hour, then expires on its own so a forgotten toggle cannot switch the product off.

The verifier runs on your own machine at https://localhost:8787. Start it with npm run server. Photos are judged by Gemma through Ollama and are never uploaded anywhere.
